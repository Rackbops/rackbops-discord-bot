# Luma generation provenance and prompts

The full illustration was generated with the built-in image generation tool, then edited with the same tool to replace its blank hanging tag with a blurple chat charm and to complete the handle with clear margins. The favicon SVGs are hand-authored reductions, not image traces.

## First concept prompt

> Create Luma, a small living courier lantern: a warm flame forming an expressive face inside an aged brass lantern cage. The handle and broad brass uprights give it a simple recognizable silhouette. A tiny blank message tag hangs near the base. Richly painted storybook gouache and ink with tactile brush marks, warm pools of light, restrained fantasy atmosphere, and elegant character design. Oxblood shadow, aged brass, amber flame, and desaturated teal patina. Full object isolated on a genuinely transparent background with clear margins. No robot, Discord logo, words, or watermark.

## Selected master edit

> Preserve the entire lantern, flame face, painterly gouache-and-ink rendering, brass materials, proportions, expression, composition, transparent background, and margins. Change only the blank tag at the lower right into a small matte blurple (`#5865F2`) rounded chat-bubble charm with two white dots. Keep it secondary, in the same position and approximate size. It is a nod to Discord conversation, not the Discord logo. No text or other additions.

## Final framing edit

> Preserve the aged brass lantern, flame face, painterly materials, two-dot charm, proportions, and transparency. Show the complete handle arc with its full outer contour and at least 10% transparent margin on all sides. Center the complete lantern in a square composition. Add no floor, shadow, scene, text, or props.

The edit completed the handle and made a square master, but did not meet the requested 10% margin. The measured transparent margins are approximately 3.9% left, 1.7% top, 1.6% right, and 1.9% bottom. Layouts using the full illustration should add their own padding.

## Future pose identity block

Use `luma.png` as the strict visual identity reference. Preserve the aged brass lantern with its broad handle and uprights, glass enclosure, warm living flame as the sole face, and small blurple two-dot chat charm hanging at the lower right. Keep the painterly gouache-and-ink texture, palette, face construction, and proportions. Change only the requested pose or expression. Show the entire lantern with real transparency and clear margins; no text, logos, extra characters, or unrelated props.
