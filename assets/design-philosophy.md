# A light that carries conversation

The bot works quietly between a community and the services it follows. Luma makes that invisible delivery visible: a small steady light inside a well-used lantern. The character should feel capable and welcoming, with a little history in its brass rather than the spotless finish of a new device.

The flame is the face. The lantern protects it and gives it a silhouette. Dark metal, warm glass, and light should carry the illustration; texture adds age but never replaces legibility. The small blurple charm is a secondary cue to the conversation space where the bot lives.

At icon scale, preserve the handle, frame, bright flame, and two eyes. Engraving, glass reflections, rope, and surface wear belong to the full illustration. A successful reduction keeps the same identity with fewer shapes.
